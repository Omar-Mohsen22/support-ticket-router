**Central question:** do the two models fail on the SAME tickets, in the SAME direction? They share no inductive bias -- bag-of-words + logistic regression (`tfidf-baseline`) versus a fine-tuned transformer (`qlora-adapter-ckpt1500`). If they nonetheless confuse the same class pairs, that is evidence the ceiling is a property of the LABELS, not of either model. If they fail differently, the ceiling is modelling capacity and there is headroom. **This report is diagnosis only -- it proposes no fix, retraining, hyperparameter, or label merge.**
